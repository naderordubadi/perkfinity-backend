Executive Summary

Perkfinity is the only loyalty and discovery platform that bridges the gap between online deal discovery and physical store redemption — all without commissions, voucher purchases, or customer data mining.

Unlike Groupon (which requires businesses to pay up to 50% commission per voucher), RetailMeNot (which focuses solely on online coupon codes), or Rakuten (which is a cash-back browser tool), Perkfinity operates as a merchant-owned member management system. Businesses pay a flat subscription fee and own their member relationships outright.

The platform supports three distinct deal flows, covering every major combination of how a business operates and how a consumer prefers to shop.

The Problem We Solve

Local businesses want to reach new customers online — but Groupon charges commissions and trains customers to expect deep discounts. Online brands want to build loyal member communities — but email platforms don't close the loop at the point of redemption. Mobile and pop-up businesses have no discovery layer at all.

Perkfinity solves all three with a single, unified platform.
